Healthcare / Booking

24/7 patient booking & front-desk agent

A single AI agent that manages appointment booking, rescheduling, and patient records for a dental clinic.

Read case study
Problem

A dental clinic's front desk couldn't answer calls and messages after hours, and appointment requests coming in through Instagram were easy to miss entirely.

What I built

One AI agent that handles booking across the website and Instagram, checks the calendar in real time, updates patient records, and sends confirmation emails automatically.

Result

Recovered an estimated 40% of after-hours booking requests that were previously going unanswered.

E-commerce / Support

Multi-channel support & order agent

One agent handling customer support and payment verification across Instagram, WhatsApp, and web chat.

Read case study
Problem

An e-commerce brand's small support team was buried in repetitive questions and manual payment-screenshot checks, spread across three separate channels.

What I built

A single AI agent that replies across Instagram, WhatsApp, and web chat in the customer's own language, reads payment screenshots to confirm orders, and logs everything to the CRM.

Result

Cut average response time by roughly 40%, freeing the support team to focus on the cases that actually needed a person.

Read case study
Problem

A creator had long-form videos sitting unused because clipping them into Shorts by hand took hours he didn't have each week.
